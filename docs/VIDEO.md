# Demo video script (~2:30)

Record the screen with OBS (or the Xbox Game Bar: Win+Alt+R) at 1080p, with the phone mirrored or filmed from above. Keep the narration in English. Do several short takes, then cut them together. Each block below is one take.

**Before recording**
- Put ~25k sats in your Selene **mainnet** wallet. The demo uses the live mainnet rally: 3 stamps × 2,000 sats, plus a member stamp at 1,800, plus fees. The 4,000-sat reward comes back on the claim.
- For the presence and organizer shots, use Selene on **chipnet**: `?net=chipnet` and the presence share link in the README.
- Open https://jelajah-bch.vercel.app in a clean browser window, zoomed to 125%.

**Tips**
- Cut away from the waits for wallet approval, and keep only the moment the stamp gets inked.
- In shot 3, slow down when Selene's signing screen shows the NFT. That is the "same transaction" proof.
- Add subtitles. Judges often watch without sound.
- Upload to YouTube (unlisted is fine) and paste the link in DoraHacks and `docs/SUBMISSION.md`.

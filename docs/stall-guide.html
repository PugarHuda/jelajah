Jelajah for stalls

Get paid in Bitcoin Cash and bring in new customers. One page, five steps.

  1. Print your posterOpen jelajah-bch.vercel.app/#/stall/<your number> and press Print this poster (A4). Tape it next to where people pay. Its QR code takes customers straight to your stall in the rally.
  2. Open the till on your phoneOn the same page, open Till QR for the stall's phone and scan it. Press Turn on the sale bell and keep the screen open. It rings the moment a stamp payment reaches the network.
  3. A customer paysThey scan the poster and press Buy in Selene or Paytaca. One wallet prompt: your price goes straight to your own wallet, and their stamp NFT is printed in the same transaction. The contract checks the price, so a short payment can't get a stamp.
  4. Check and serveThe till shows the amount and the buyer. ⏳ unconfirmed turns final in about 10 minutes; for a small order you can serve right away, for a big one wait for it.
  5. Keep your recordsPress Download sales (CSV) on the till any time. Every number comes from the chain, so it matches what anyone else can count on #/stats.
Why customers come back
Stamps from 3 different stalls pay them a reward from the prize pool and mint a trophy. The trophy is a member card: a lower price at every stall, checked by the contract.
Why the prize is safe
Each stamp pays a small levy into the pool, and each set's own levies pay its reward. Stalls that fake purchases between themselves only pay themselves. No one holds the pool: a contract does.
